# Papercut

A **papercut** is one small thing that slowed you down. Report it in a minute, then go back to your
task. One occurrence is enough. Your part is the report; the user's retro decides what to change.

1. **Find the Papercuts project.** In Linear, find the project named `Papercuts` that belongs to the
   project you are working in, by that name. A pointer your project gives, such as its ID, is a shortcut.
2. **Search it.** If this papercut is already there, comment your case on it in the fields below.
   Repeat comments are its severity. Then go to step 5.
3. **File it.** Otherwise file one short ticket in that project, titled with the symptom:
   - **Tried**: what you were doing.
   - **Expected**: what you expected to happen.
   - **Happened**: the exact output line.
   - **Suggested fix**: one line.
   - **Skill**: the skill or doc in play, if any.
   - Sign it `<role>/model: <id>`.
4. **Linear unreachable?** Put the same fields under a `Papercut` heading in your final report.
5. **Return to your task.**
