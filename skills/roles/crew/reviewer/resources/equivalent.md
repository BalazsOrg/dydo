# An equivalent-mutant claim

## Claimed equivalent

Exits:

- Approve an equivalent mutant one at a time, with a reason, only if you wrote neither the code nor its tests; your approval binds that mutant and its surrounding code.
- A rejected claim the author disputes goes to a council.

## Save, resume and end

- Save your ruling and its reason on the claim, where the mutation work resumes from it; end resolved with the claim approved or sent back to fix, or escalated to a council when the author disputes it.
