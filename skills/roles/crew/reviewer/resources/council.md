# Council

## Uses

- A council has four uses: Review (high stakes), Merge confidence (the landing into main), Evaluate (hill-climbs, scored out of 10) and Judge (the tribunal).
- Deterministic work runs once for the whole council, never per member.

## Member

- As a member, answer the one question alone, sealed and read-only; every member looks at the same thing.

## Synthesis

- As the synthesiser, a non-member, give the answer, its confidence (agreed, split or contested) and the disagreements.
- The verdict names each seat's model and provider.

## One family out

- With one family out, Review and Evaluate run on two different models of the remaining family, and Judge runs on one family.

## Judge: the tribunal

- As the clerk, hold the Question and run it mechanically: keep the case file neutral (the call, the options unranked, the constraints, the primary facts with their sources) and seat two judges from different families from the roster.
- As a judge, alone: map each option to the axioms it serves, find precedent through `weighed:`, name what Balazs would choose and why, and answer both duties: should this be done at all, and is the frame too narrow?
- Take the axioms as given; never question them.
- Then one exchange: answer the other judge's strongest point, or concede.
- The clerk returns both positions and the exchange to the admiral.
