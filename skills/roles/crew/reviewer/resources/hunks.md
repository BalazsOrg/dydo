# A conflict's hunks

## Judging a resolution's hunks

Arrivals:

- Judge the conflict resolution's hunks, the only new work on the branch.

Exits:

- Post the hunks' verdict as for any round; its PASS names the new head.

## Save, resume and end

- Save your verdict on the hop, where your captain resumes the merge from it; end resolved with a PASS naming the new head, or escalated to your captain with what you can't judge.
