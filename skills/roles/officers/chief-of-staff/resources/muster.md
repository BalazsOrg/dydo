# Muster

Hold this checklist with him present, after `overnight-mode`'s transition preflight; the night starts only once every line is done.

- Ask each live coordinator for its muster report, with the request below.
- Resolve each operational blocker while he is present, verifying each fix with evidence.
- Exercise each permission the night's work needs through a safe, authorised operation where one exists, such as contacting the coordinator, each session's Linear writes and feature-branch merges, and record the real result; an unprotected branch proves no merge permission, and a dummy merge is never run.
- Keep an exercise you can't run, or an item left unresolved, as a gap, and get his explicit disposition for the work that depends on it.
- A stop line he wants goes in the Project plan, which its coordinator reads.
- Get each coordinator's confirmation that its next work is runnable.
- List for him each coordinator's thread link and its readiness in the form below, never a report's content; then the muster is ready.

**Questions for him**

- Put every question you ask him at muster, whatever its source, as one block in the `grilling` skill's format, shown in the question block below.
- Give in the block everything he needs to answer and has not seen, even what a coordinator's report already says: he answers from the block alone, without opening a thread.
- Give every option, each with its trade-off: at least two, and all that the question's source named.
- Recommend one of those options after `➡️`, with your reason.
- Verify each premise the question rests on before you show it, and link the evidence in the block: a premise is anything the block gives as fact, what an option can do and its trade-off included.
- Hold a question back while a fact its answer depends on is unverified, each option's trade-off included: verify it, or send the question back to its source to verify, and tell him in one line which question waits and on what.
- Where no record and no check open to you or to the question's source verifies a fact its answer depends on, the question stays held; where at least two ways to act have trade-offs verified without that fact, ask him a question of your own, how to act without it: say in the block what is unknown, and give only those ways as options.
- Write the block in plain language: define a term he has not used where it first appears, or replace it with plain words that mean the same.
- Before a question that asks him to spend money or grant a permission, check for a route that needs neither, and say in the block what you checked and what it showed.

**Question block**

```text
❓ **Q1** - **<question title>**: <what he has not seen, each premise with its evidence link, then every option with its trade-off>

➡️ <the option you recommend, and your reason>

---

❓ **Q2** - **<question title>**: <the same, for the next question>

➡️ <the option you recommend, and your reason>
```

**Request to each coordinator**

```text
His muster request for <date>, <Project>. Return:
Intent: his goals and purpose for the Project in your own words, by /in-your-own-words, apart from the work.
Work: your next tickets in fill order, the first runnable one, and the plan's stop line, or none.
Blockers: each operational obstacle, and the evidence that would show it fixed.
Permissions: each action the night's work needs, with a safe exercise for it, or the gap.
```

**List for him**

```text
<Project> — <coordinator thread link> — <ready, or not ready>
```
