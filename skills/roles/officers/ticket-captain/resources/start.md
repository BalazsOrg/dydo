# Before you start

- Your brief or the ticket's contract lacks something the work needs, such as who merges, the paths you own or the gates you must pass: ask your officer for exactly that, by the step of [A need for the user](need-for-the-user.md) that sends to your officer's thread, and start nothing that depends on it.
- The ticket is already started, or closed, and your brief does not say that you take it over: another agent holds it, or held it. Start nothing, and return the dispatch to your officer as a conflict, with the ticket's key and its status.
- Your brief calls the ticket filler: if it is not small, reversible and free of design calls, return it to your officer unstarted and unassigned, with a one-line reason.
