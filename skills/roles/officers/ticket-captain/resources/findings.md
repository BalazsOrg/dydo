# A finding's route

**A blocker, by what it shows:**

- Work that is broken, against the spec or a security hole: the round's one Fix ticket, by [Fix rounds](fix-rounds.md).
- A fundamental fault, or a new requirement: your officer, for a re-plan.
- A need only the user can meet: your officer, at once, by [A need for the user](need-for-the-user.md).

**A should, by what its fix costs:**

- Cheap: have it fixed before the merge. Name it in the round's Fix brief; when the round has no blocker, dispatch a writer onto its ticket, then go by where the candidate is. In your PR: dispatch a Review onto the new head, since `dydo merge` takes no head without a PASS. This round is not the one more you may order, and you take it once: a should that it, or a later round with no blocker, finds waits on its ticket. In no PR: dispatch none, since the whole's Review will judge the mended head. If that head is the one your officer merges, go on to phase 5 of the spine and return its SHA.
- Anything more: it waits on its ticket, and its parent may close Done meanwhile.

Take a finding on a change you wrote as the reviewer wrote it: its Fix is never yours to write, and goes out by [Fix rounds](fix-rounds.md); if you doubt the finding, the doubt is a Question sub-ticket, never your call.
