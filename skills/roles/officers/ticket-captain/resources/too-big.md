# Too big to hold

1. Commit what is whole, and post its SHA on your ticket.
2. Divide what is left yourself, into sub-tickets that each hold together and start from that SHA, and dispatch them.
3. If what is left cannot be divided coherently under your ticket, for example because part of it belongs with a sibling's work, return `too big, re-cut` to your officer, naming what is done and what is left. Your ticket then stays open as it stands: your officer settles it, and you do nothing more on it.

When a child returns `too big, re-cut`, the cut is yours, and so is the child's ticket, in this order:

1. Settle the child's ticket on what it finished at the SHA it returned: rewrite its contract, in the ticket's description, down to that, and close the ticket Done on it; if the child finished nothing whole, cancel it instead. Note the re-cut on the ticket in one line.
2. Open sub-tickets for what it left, starting from that SHA.

A change to your contract, or to a sibling's, is never part of your cut: take it to your officer.
