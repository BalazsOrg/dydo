# Too big to hold

1. Commit what is whole, and post its SHA on your ticket.
2. Divide what is left yourself, into sub-tickets that each hold together and start from that SHA, and dispatch them.
3. If what is left cannot be divided coherently under your ticket, for example because part of it belongs with a sibling's work, return `too big, re-cut` to your officer, naming what is done and what is left.

When a child returns `too big, re-cut`, the cut is yours: open sub-tickets for what it left, starting from the SHA it returned.
A change to your contract, or to a sibling's, is never part of your cut: take it to your officer.
