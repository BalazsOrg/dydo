# Too big to hold

1. Commit what is whole, and post its SHA on your ticket.
2. Cut what is left into sub-tickets that start from that SHA, and dispatch them.
3. If you cannot hold the split either, return `too big, re-cut` to your officer, naming what is done and what is left.

When a child returns `too big, re-cut`, the cut is yours: open sub-tickets for what it left, starting from the SHA it returned.
