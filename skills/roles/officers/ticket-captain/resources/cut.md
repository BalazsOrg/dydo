# Cut

Your ticket needs more than one agent. You cut it into sub-tickets, one for each part. From now on you only orchestrate: other agents do the work.

**Cut the parts in sight**

- Cut only the parts whose contract you can write now. Leave the rest uncut until a part comes back: what it found shows you the next parts. Never cut the whole at once.
- Size each part by "Size it" in the `ticket-captain` skill: each part fits one agent.
- Join a piece too small to be worth a brief to the part it belongs with.
- A call you may not decide is a part of its own, a Question. Open and dispatch it by steps 1 and 2 of [Ask](ask.md). You wait on it as on any other part.
- When you can see ahead a call that only the user can make, cut its Question first. Such a call is no agent's job, and the user's recorded words, the spec and the statechart, where the project has one, leave it open. The answer is then there before work is built on it.
- To keep wrong work from being built on, you may cut a review of a part as one more part.
- When no part is in sight, cut one part that brings the next parts into sight: a Question, a prototype, or a Wayfinding ticket.

**Write each sub-ticket**

The sub-ticket's description is its contract. Write there:

- the outcome, as something a reader can check;
- how it fits your ticket;
- the parts of the spec it serves, quoted.

**Dispatch**

Dispatch every part that waits on no other part, each by [Brief](brief.md). Then go by [Wait](wait.md).

**The work cannot be cut inside your ticket**

A part may belong with another ticket's work, or the cut may need your contract changed. Write why on your ticket, and end with `handed back <KEY>: <reason>`.
