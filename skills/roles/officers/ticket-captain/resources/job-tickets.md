# Job tickets

## Question

- Every Question carries a stage label, set by whoever opens it: `research` and `council` are Mode AFK, `his-call` Mode HITL, and the stage, not the parent's Type, picks how it is held.
- At stage `research`, research the call first with `research` crew; open a `council` or `his-call` child only when the research does not resolve it.
- A research Question you open under another Question stays at stage `research`.
- On a `his-call` child you open, write the envoy brief's seven slots: the ticket and why it needs him · what's settled · sources to read first · the stop line · where to record, in his words · return and closing summary · the opening rule, a fresh 2–3-line picture, then the first question with its recommendation.

## A Question's call, after its research

Nominal path:

- A `dr-grade` call goes to a `his-call` child, except overnight, when a two-way, seriously blocking one goes to a `council` child.

Exits:

- A call a DR clearly settles is not hard: decide it and cite the DR.
- Open a `his-call` child only when the call is worth his time and attention to understand; a rubber-stamp question never reaches him.
- Otherwise open a `council` child for a hard call, by day or night: a DR and the spec disagree, nuance may change a DR or the spec, or there is no clear answer.

## Wayfinding

- Holding a Wayfinding ticket, you are the deciding half of the Project's map holder: the coordinator keeps the map on track, mechanically, and every call on it is yours.
- Write and refine `Spec: <Project>`, the Project's main Linear document with C8's headings: problem, behaviour, decisions, out of scope, acceptance checks; a sub-feature that needs defining well gets its own spec, linked from it.
- Give every feature a spec, whether or not a DR backs it.
- When the spec changes, reconcile the open contracts with it.
- Re-chart as fog clears and cut the tickets the map needs, deciding each cut the coordinator would otherwise face; file what you cut and return, and the coordinator only sequences it.
- Contract each `uncontracted` Backlog ticket, so it moves to Todo.
- While charting, place the Project's Milestones in Linear: few, each a chunk where drift from his intent would show, the walking skeleton first and the landing last; every delivery ticket belongs to one.
- File the walking skeleton's Walkthrough with the map, as a native blocker of the next Milestone's tickets until his verdict or skip; later Milestones' Walkthroughs block nothing.
- A seat you record on a ticket you cut replaces the coordinator's fixed rule for it: apply the same rule unless you record why not.
- A filler ticket blocked on yours stays unpullable until you record its release on your ticket.
- When a release is planned, harvest the adopters' Agent friction queues and local patches.
- At the landing, once he accepts, move the spec's lasting parts to DRs, the glossary, docs and the verification map, then delete the spec and the plan files.

## With him: Grilling, Walkthrough, Enablement and his-call

- Changes during a session with him reach you as dispatcher updates; never ask him to repeat them.
- After a shared understanding, and once you have reported back, tell him the thread can be closed.
- Record the result on your ticket, in his words.
- Holding a bundle's wrapper, load every job skill its members need: `wizard` for an Enablement, `co-thinker` for a `his-call` Question.
