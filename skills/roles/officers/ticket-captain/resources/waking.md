# Waking

A ticket you set In Progress yourself, earlier in this thread, is yours: its status is no conflict, and you need no take-over line.

1. Re-read your ticket, your sub-tickets' statuses and the last SHA posted on each, and go on from the phase you had reached: the record, never your memory of it, says where things stand.
2. Post the SHA a child returns on its ticket, to resume from; a return of `too big, re-cut` then goes by [Too big to hold](too-big.md).
3. Send what a child reports outside its scope to the owner's ticket or a new Bug.
4. Cancel a sub-ticket you opened ahead that the return made unnecessary, such as the Fix of a round that passed, with a one-line reason: a Canceled step blocks nothing.
5. Dispatch each AFK sub-ticket that nothing blocks any more: every sub-ticket that blocked it is Done or Canceled.
