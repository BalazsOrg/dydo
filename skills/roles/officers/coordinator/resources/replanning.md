# A fundamental blocker or a new requirement

## Re-planning after a fundamental blocker or a new requirement

Arrivals:

- A fundamental blocker or a new requirement from below: open a Wayfinding ticket to re-plan the affected part of the map, and put a fresh captain on it.

Exits:

- Once the re-plan is filed and its dead hops are Canceled with a one-line reason, sequence what it filed and go back to watching.

## Save, resume and end

- Save the blocker or requirement on the Wayfinding ticket and resume watching from what it files; end resolved once its re-plan is sequenced, or escalated as a `research` Question when its captain passes a need up.
