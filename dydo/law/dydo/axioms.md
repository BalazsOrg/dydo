# dydo Axioms

The seven axioms, in their original order. Text unchanged; named slugs approved in [DECIDED, Part B](https://linear.app/bodnar-balazs/issue/DYD-1078#comment-9ac278c0-00f3-4688-9971-2693bfc14e38).

## dydo-scarce-attention

**The user can't scale; agents can.** His attention is the scarce resource.

## dydo-aligned-intent

**Align intent.** The system is an extension of the user's will: his values, what he thinks is good
   code, and what he thinks is good aesthetically. His intent must be elicited, then reflected everywhere.

## dydo-finite-context

**An agent's context is finite.** A full context gets dumber or gets compacted, and either way it
   works worse.

## dydo-everything-evolves

**Everything evolves.** Agents, tools and platforms keep getting better. The system stays modular and
   light so that something new never strands it.

## dydo-efficiency

**Efficiency.** The best result for the time and tokens spent.

## dydo-easy-path

**Agents are set up for success.** The easy path is the right path.

## dydo-verify-own-work

**Agents can verify their own work.** Consequence: by the time a PR reaches the user, he has very high
   confidence it is correct, because the problems were caught along the way.
